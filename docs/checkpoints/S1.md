## 1. Why (the gap)

The harness scores **content reconstruction** — `simMae`, independent MAE and
block-SSIM (`js/engine/metrics.js`, `js/engine/score.js`). Those reward *"the
render looks like the photo"*, not *"the stroke language matches the
references"*. Before a Style-Match Score (S2) can exist, a reference first needs
a machine-readable description of its own stroke language: where strokes are
large vs small, which way they run, and how strongly. That is S1.

## 2. Design

`analyzeReference(source, opts) → RefPacket`, **additive** — it consumes
`analyzer.js` (structure tensor: orientation/coherence/energy) and `masks.js`
(tonal bands) and changes neither.

- **Adaptive grid.** `chooseGridSize()` aims for ~`targetCell` (24 px) cells and
  clamps to `[8, 32]`, so small images get 8×8 and large ones up to 32×32.
- **Per cell:** energy-weighted circular mean of the tangent `orientation`
  (PI-periodic, double-angle), `orientationStrength` (resultant length R),
  `coherence`, `energy`, `density` (fraction of pixels with structure above a
  threshold), `contrast` (local luminance σ/255), `luma`, and `strokeScale`.
- **Implied stroke scale** (relative units, larger = coarser): a busy cell (many
  edges + high local contrast) wants fine strokes, a flat cell coarse —
  `strokeScale = 1 − clamp01(0.65·density + 0.35·clamp01(2.5·contrast))`, floored
  at `scaleFloor` (0.08).
- **Summaries:** `scaleHistogram` (8 bins, counts cells), `strokeScaleByBand`
  (mean scale per tonal band, band-mass weighted), `orientationField`
  (12-bin energy-weighted histogram + dominant + strength), `coverage`
  / `depthProxy` (tonal / paper-vs-subject), `stats`.
- **JSON-safe.** No typed arrays in the packet, so S3 can store a fused packet
  on `profile.provenance` and it round-trips through `JSON.parse(JSON.stringify)`.
- **Deterministic.** No randomness beyond `analyzer.js`'s seeded palette.

result's RefPacket against the reference's (stroke-scale histogram distance +
